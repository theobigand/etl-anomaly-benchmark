Image language: technical macro still life photographed like a surface-inspection record. Truthful and explanatory, quiet, not advertising, not sci-fi.

Subject world: a machined dark graphite anodized aluminium inspection plate carrying a perfectly regular grid of small square raised pads (each pad 6 mm square, 0.4 mm tall, matte, fine machined grain, crisp tiny chamfer).
