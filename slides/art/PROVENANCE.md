Physical set-up stated before generation: one low warm source from the left at about 12 degrees,
shadows to the right, shadow length proportional to pad height. Checked afterwards: the raised pad's
shadow falls right and is long; ordinary pads cast short shadows; the cracked pad sits flush and casts
none. Residual tells: the camera looks almost straight down rather than at 35 degrees, the plate reads
brown-bronze rather than graphite, and the 1672 px masters are soft when a crop is enlarged (the openers
use 150 to 240 percent crops of the cover plate, shown at 1280 px).

## Data behind native figures (2026-11-12)

- `data/pr_curves.json`: precision-recall curves of every detector on Credit Card Fraud, Water Quality, Cardio and the
  synthetic set, written by `tooling/export_pr_curves.py`, which calls `experiments/run_curves.py::scores_for` (same
  datasets, same seed 42, same split and detectors). Curves are down-sampled to 0.0025 recall bins (mean precision per
  bin); the first vertex (recall 0, precision 1) is kept. AP values are the unrounded `average_precision_score`.
  Against the legend of the archived `paper/figures/pr_curves.png` the regenerated AP matches for 39 of 41 curves;
  PCA differs on Water Quality (0.54 against 0.57) and on the synthetic set (0.48 against 0.56). The archived figure
  predates later code changes and also reused one colour for two methods (ten colours, eleven curves). The slide uses
  the regenerated values and says so; the paper figure is unchanged.
- Severity curves are read from `verification/evidence/severity.csv` (mean over three seeds, synthetic).
