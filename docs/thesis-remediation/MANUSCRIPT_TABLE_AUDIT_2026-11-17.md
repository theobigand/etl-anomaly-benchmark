## What changed and why

- **Compact view (new Appendix C, `tab:compact`).** Four consecutive 17-row matrices were the densest
  stretch of the manuscript. A 6 by 4 table now gives the best detector of each type on each dataset and
  precedes them, so the claim that each method has a narrow competence can be checked at a glance and the
  full matrices serve as evidence. Every cell repeats a maximum of a column of the generated matrices.
- **Reproducible and checkable.** `paper/compact_view.py` rewrites the block between its markers from the
  matrices and nothing else; `python paper/compact_view.py --check` exits 1 when the block is stale. The
  generators of the matrices and of the pool extension (`research/paper_appendix.py`,
  `research/pool_extension_paper.py`) were not touched, so a rebuild overwrites nothing.
- **Navigation.** The results paragraph that discusses the per-type structure points to the compact view
  and to the matrices; the opening of the detailed-results appendix says which tables answer which question
  and where the conclusion is stated. Letters and numbers follow from `\ref`, so references stay valid.
- **Not done, on purpose.** No table was turned into a figure: the figures that exist are generated by
  `experiments/` scripts that are under mutation governance, and this closure freezes those roots. No
  count target was applied; a table stays when it is the clearest and most rigorous representation.
