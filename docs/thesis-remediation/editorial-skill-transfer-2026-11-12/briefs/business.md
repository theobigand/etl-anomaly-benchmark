# Brief C (business/operational): quarterly operating review
Audience: executive committee, decision meeting. Deliver a 6-slide deck.
Subject: Q3 2026 operating review of a regional bakery chain, Boulangerie Lenoir, 14 shops (fictional).
Facts: revenue EUR 4.82 M (+6.1% against Q3 2025). Gross margin 61.4% (down 1.2 points). Waste 8.7% of production value (target 6.0%). Staff turnover 24% annualised. Per-shop revenue range EUR 210k to EUR 520k; five shops below EUR 260k (Gare, Lycée, Zone Nord, Halles, Marais), all five have waste above 10%. New oven programme: 3 ovens replaced, energy cost per baguette -9%. Flour price up 11% since January; contract renegotiation due 1 December.
Decisions requested: (1) approve closing the 16:30 to 19:00 slot in the three weakest shops (saves EUR 118k a year, loses estimated EUR 41k revenue), (2) approve EUR 260k for 4 more ovens, (3) mandate procurement to hedge 60% of flour volume for 2027.
Risks: closing slots may raise turnover; hedging locks price if flour falls.
