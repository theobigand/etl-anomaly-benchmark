# Notes

**Art direction.** Data-led, operational role for an executive decision meeting. Typography and evidence carry the deck: Iowan Old Style titles and numerals over Avenir Next, ruled ledgers instead of card grids. Dark rye opener and closer frame the light evidence slides (verdict and ask versus proof).

**Imagery.** None. Each slide's job is a number, a range or a decision, so photography would not carry evidence. Image brief if wanted later: raking-light photograph of a baguette tray.

**Palette.** Derived from the subject: flour paper (#f5efe3), rye-crust browns (#201a14, #3a2c21), crust orange (#9a4a1c) as the single editorial signal, brick red for adverse and sage green for favourable values only.

**Skill instructions followed.** DESIGN_SYSTEM (type stacks, sizes, spacing), ART_DIRECTION (visual role, opening structure, essential visuals: range strip, waste bars, saving versus lost revenue bars), VOICE (point-stating titles, no em dashes, derived values labelled, missing data stated once), PRESENTATION_RULES (one idea per slide, 1280x720 fit, no shrunk text). Every slide rendered and inspected; two revision passes. Derived figures: 65k per oven, 62.6% prior margin, 2.7 point waste gap.
