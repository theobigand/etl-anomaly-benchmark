A small ceramics studio opens its autumn collection: cups, serving bowls and lamp bases, numbered by hand.

Opening evening, Friday 14 November 2026, Quai des Salines
Illustration, not to scale. Left to right: Galet, Estran, Vigie.

Local clay, glaze mixed from seaweed ash and wood ash

Two potters work in a converted fish-smokehouse. The clay comes from a quarry nearby. The glazes are mixed in the studio from two ashes, one from seaweed and one from wood.

The Galet cups are fired at 1280 C.
