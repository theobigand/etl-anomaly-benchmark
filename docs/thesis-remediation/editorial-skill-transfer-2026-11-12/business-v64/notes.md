# Notes

**Art direction.** Role: data-led and operational, for an executive decision meeting. Visual thesis: a printed management ledger. Large serif figures carry each claim, hairline rules organise rows, semantic colour only (red = adverse, green = favourable, crust orange = decision numbering). Opening: a dark cover with a figures panel, chosen because the committee needs the result and the three asks on one page, not a photo with a title. Slides vary by role: ledger, scale diagram, two-sided comparison, decision table, risk pair.

**Imagery.** None. An image would not do a job a chart cannot: the shop spread, waste vs target and the decision economics are exact quantities. No stock bakery photography, to avoid decoration. No image brief needed.

**Palette.** Derived from the subject: flour paper (#f6f0e3), rye-crust ink (#241a13), baked-crust accent (#a9531a). Status red and green are muted for contrast on paper. Light mode only (projected meeting).

**Data integrity.** Only brief facts. Derived: 2.7 pts waste gap, EUR 77k difference, 2.5 hour slot, 40% unhedged. Per-shop values other than 210k and 520k are not drawn.
